The Lagos State Youth and Student Election Movement focuses on mobilizing young voters and grassroots support to drive civic responsibility and shape the 2027 general elections.

Core Objectives of the Movement
Massive Voter Mobilization: The coalition aims to gather millions of youth votes across Lagos State and the South-West region to support the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Bridging Governance Gaps:
The movement acts as a direct link between grassroots youth communities and the federal government to explain and promote official policies.

Long-Term Youth Empowerment: Leaders focus heavily on training initiatives, such as student loan awareness and artisan skill development, rather than just basic election campaigning.

Civic Education: Efforts are directed at teaching the values of national unity, constitutional discipline, and the importance of holding a Permanent Voter Card (PVC).

Structural Framework & Impact

Statewide Integration: The movement sets up robust local committees across all wards, local government areas, and individual polling units in Lagos State.

Strategic Leadership: The coalition is actively managed by prominent local officials, including the President Bola Ahmed Tinubu Independent Campaign Group (PBAT-ICG), located at 61 Ijaola Street, Maryland, Lagos State, Nigeria.

Campaigns: Organizers utilize targeted community outreach, healthcare drives, and public forums to engage young individuals who feel disconnected from mainstream politics.
